BACKGROUND
The folder data/tennis_atp/ holds ATP tennis match files: atp_matches_1968.csv .. atp_matches_2026.csv, atp_players.csv, atp_rankings_*.csv, LICENSE and UPSTREAM_README.md. They were downloaded by fetch_data.sh from https://github.com/Aneeshers/tennis-sackmann-archive (its atp/ folder), a mirror whose README says it snapshots Jeff Sackmann's https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_atp from a June 2026 commit. The original repository now returns 404. The Wayback Machine has a capture at http://web.archive.org/web/20260314214431/https://github.com/JeffSackmann/tennis_atp . Python with pandas and pyarrow is available as python3 and as "<repo>/.venv/bin/python".

GOAL
Establish the provenance of these files and verify them against sources independent of the mirror.

TASKS
A1 Chain of custody. From the mirror repository (README, commit history, any metadata) identify the upstream commit (hash and date) its atp/ snapshot was taken from. Find at least one surviving fork of JeffSackmann/tennis_atp on GitHub (forks persist after the parent is deleted; use the GitHub search API, e.g. https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=tennis_atp+in:name , or web search) whose last upstream commit is at or after that date. For the seasons 1991, 2000, 2010, 2019, 2024 and 2025 compare the local file with the fork's raw file: row count, number of columns, sha256. Download individual raw files only, never a whole repository; keep total downloads under 60 MB. Also compare whatever row counts or file sizes are visible in the Wayback capture. Report identical or different, with the numbers.
A2 Spot-check 20 matches against an independent public source (the ATP Tour website, Wikipedia match articles, or similar): locate each match in the local files by player names and year, then compare winner, score, and both players' aces and double faults. The 15 required matches: 2019 Wimbledon final Djokovic v Federer; 2008 Wimbledon final Nadal v Federer; 2012 Australian Open final Djokovic v Nadal; 2010 Wimbledon first round Isner v Mahut; 2001 Wimbledon fourth round Federer v Sampras; 1991 US Open final Edberg v Courier; 1995 Wimbledon final Sampras v Becker; 2004 Roland Garros final Gaudio v Coria; 2009 Wimbledon final Federer v Roddick; 2014 US Open final Cilic v Nishikori; 2017 Australian Open final Federer v Nadal; 2020 US Open final Thiem v Zverev; 2022 Roland Garros final Nadal v Ruud; 2023 Wimbledon final Alcaraz v Djokovic; 2024 US Open final Sinner v Fritz. Choose 5 more Grand Slam or Masters 1000 finals from the 2025 and 2026 season files.
A3 Confirm that LICENSE is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and state what attribution it requires.

ACCEPTANCE
A Markdown report with sections A1, A2 (a 20-row table: match, local values, source values, agree yes/no, source URL), A3, a chain-of-custody narrative, and a one-line verdict CONFIRMED / PARTIALLY CONFIRMED / NOT CONFIRMED with reasons. Write it to verification/antigravity/provenance/provenance_report.md and print the full report to stdout at the end.

SCOPE (must follow)
Write only inside verification/antigravity/provenance/. Do not open, read or modify atp_sim/, tests/, scripts/, runs/ or any project file other than data/tennis_atp/*, fetch_data.sh and files under verification/. Do not modify any data file.
