BACKGROUND
A training table for a tennis serve model has been built from the raw ATP files in data/tennis_atp/ (atp_matches_1991.csv .. atp_matches_2026.csv and atp_players.csv). The outputs are runs/rows.parquet, runs/cards.parquet, runs/constants.csv and runs/build_report.md. The complete specification is verification/VERIFY_SPEC.md. A separate independent audit of the raw files is in verification/antigravity/raw_audit/raw_audit.md and its script raw_audit.py may be reused for the validity rules. Python with pandas and pyarrow is available as python3 and as "<repo>/.venv/bin/python". You do not have, and must not look for, the code that produced the outputs: recompute everything from the raw files and the specification alone.

GOAL
Independently verify that the outputs follow VERIFY_SPEC.md exactly, and report PASS or FAIL per check with the numbers behind it.

CHECKS
C1 Draw a random sample of 1,000 rows from rows.parquet with a seed you choose and print. For each sampled row recompute from the raw files, per the spec, the server's card and the returner's card at that row's tourney_date: n_52w, n_10 and the 12 window sums (svpt_52, svwon_52, ace_52, df_52, bpf_52, bps_52, rpt_52, rwon_52, obpf_52, obps_52, svpt_10, svwon_10) as exact integers; the raw rates, shrunk rates and form (absolute difference at most 1e-9, NaN equal to NaN); age (at most 1e-9, NaN equal to NaN); and, using mu and sigma from constants.csv for the row's season, the eight x values (at most 1e-6). Compare with cards.parquet and with the row's x_i_* and x_j_* columns. Report how many rows agree and list every disagreement.
C2 Recompute from the raw files the priors and constants (m_prior, mu, sigma, n_pop for every attr) for seasons 1995, 2010 and 2024 exactly as sections 6 and 9 define them, and compare with constants.csv: m_prior and mu within 1e-9, sigma within relative 1e-8, n_pop exact.
C3 Delete-the-future: for 50 cards sampled from cards.parquet with season >= 1992, rebuild the card from a copy of the raw data with every match dated on or after the card's date removed; it must equal the stored card in every column named in C1, same tolerances. Control: repeat with only the matches dated strictly after the date removed (same-day matches kept) and confirm that at least one card changes; if none changes, say so and explain why.
C4 Invariants on the full table: (a) the number of rows equals twice the number of valid matches (section 3) with season >= 1992 and surface in Hard, Clay, Grass; (b) every match_id appears exactly twice with server_id and returner_id swapped; (c) svpt and won equal the raw file's values for the server; (d) the column set equals section 10 exactly and no column name matches the regex win|los|score|rank|minutes|name|seed; (e) every x value is finite; (f) no row has surface Carpet or missing, season below 1992, or a tourney_name matching Next ?Gen|Laver Cup; (g) within each match_id the two rows are ordered by server_id; (h) every (server_id, tourney_date) exists in cards.parquet; (i) cards with n_52w = 0 have shrunk rates equal to m_prior for their season and form = 0; (j) rows with i_dob_missing true have x_i_7 = 0; (k) per season and attribute, the mean and population SD of x_i_*: report them all and flag any |mean| above 0.5 or SD outside 0.7 to 1.3; (l) for each season Y from 1993 to 2026, standardising P(Y-1) as defined in section 9 with season Y's constants gives mean 0 and SD 1 within 1e-9 for every attribute whose sigma is not 0.
Note: constants.csv also holds a row for season 2027 (constants for forecasting, from P(2026)); rows.parquet has no 2027 rows. That is expected, not a defect.

ACCEPTANCE
verification/antigravity/table_check/table_check.md with a PASS or FAIL line per check (C1, C2, C3, C4a to C4l) followed by the numbers behind it, the seeds, and every disagreement listed; the complete script saved as verification/antigravity/table_check/table_check.py; the report printed in full to stdout at the end. The overall verdict is PASS only if every check passes.

SCOPE (must follow)
Write only inside verification/antigravity/table_check/. Read only data/tennis_atp/*, runs/rows.parquet, runs/cards.parquet, runs/constants.csv, runs/build_report.md and files under verification/. Do not open, read or modify atp_sim/, tests/, scripts/ or any other project file. Do not modify the data or runs files. Do not install packages. Do not use the network. Keep the total runtime under 15 minutes: vectorise the recomputation, and reduce the C1 sample to 300 rows if 1,000 would take longer.
